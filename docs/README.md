# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation explains how OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, and integrations.

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative approach with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and a culture of feedback and learning. Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize the backlog; developers, QA, and stakeholders collaborate on implementation, quality, and approvals.

The project lifecycle covers initiation (define the problem, stakeholders, and high-level timeline), planning (agree scope, resources, milestones, and dependencies), execution and tracking (build, test, review, and iterate), release and deployment (deploy, verify, and announce), and retrospectives and continuous improvement (capture learnings and next steps). Risk management and communication support the entire lifecycle through a risk register, regular team and stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations.

Key artifacts include a project charter, roadmap and release plan, iteration backlog, acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, risk register, and retrospective action items.
